About

My name is Nomel ESMEL, a 3D Generalist currently in my second year of the Master’s program at IIM Digital School (La Défense). I specialize in environments, props and stylized animation, from modeling through to final render.

I'm currently seeking a 4 to 6-month end-of-studies internship starting February 23, 2026, in an animation or 3D/CGI studio (film, games, advertising, archviz). My goal: join a team where I can contribute to production while leveling up in staging, lighting and visual storytelling.

I love clean pipelines, teamwork and technical problem-solving. Outside of class, I’m developing a personal narrative universe (“Imaginarium”) and exploring Blender / Unreal / Substance workflows to streamline production.
