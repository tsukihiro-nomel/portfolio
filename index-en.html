About

Hello! I'm Nomel ESMEL, a detail-driven 3D generalist — the type who will tweak a shader until it reacts exactly the way I pictured it. I grew up in Côte d’Ivoire, passed my baccalauréat there and then joined IIM Digital School, where I discovered 3D. I'm now in my fifth year (Master’s in 3D Animation Directing) with the same drive: building stories, learning relentlessly and bringing worlds to life.

I love touching every part of the pipeline: modeling, texturing, shading, animation — the full generalist playground. Whether it's a coffee cup or a complex character, I focus on coherence and emotion. Those projects also taught me to stay rigorous, react quickly and remain calm when a render crashes at 2 a.m.

Creativity does not stop when I close the software. Manga sharpen my storytelling instincts, cooking teaches patience and balance, and piano keeps my sense of rhythm alive. These passions fuel how I mix technique and emotion.

I'm currently looking for a dynamic team where I can contribute to real productions while refining my craft. If you're into passionate, hands-on profiles with a healthy dose of determination, I'd love to chat.
